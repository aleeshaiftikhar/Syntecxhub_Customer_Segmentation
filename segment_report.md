
# Customer Segmentation Report

## Project Overview
This project uses K-Means clustering to segment mall customers based on age, annual income, and spending score. The Mall Customers dataset contains 200 records. The Elbow Method was used to help select five clusters.

## Cluster 0: Low-Income, Low-Spending Customers
- Customers: 20
- Average Age: 46.25
- Average Annual Income: 26.75 k$
- Average Spending Score: 18.35
- Marketing Strategy: Offer budget-friendly products, discounts, and seasonal promotions.

## Cluster 1: Young, Active-Spending Customers
- Customers: 54
- Average Age: 25.19
- Average Annual Income: 41.09 k$
- Average Spending Score: 62.24
- Marketing Strategy: Use social media campaigns, loyalty rewards, and attractive product offers.

## Cluster 2: High-Income, High-Spending Customers
- Customers: 40
- Average Age: 32.88
- Average Annual Income: 86.10 k$
- Average Spending Score: 81.53
- Marketing Strategy: Provide premium products, VIP rewards, and personalized shopping offers.

## Cluster 3: High-Income, Low-Spending Customers
- Customers: 39
- Average Age: 39.87
- Average Annual Income: 86.10 k$
- Average Spending Score: 19.36
- Marketing Strategy: Offer targeted promotions, product recommendations, and incentives to encourage purchases.

## Cluster 4: Older, Moderate-Spending Customers
- Customers: 47
- Average Age: 55.64
- Average Annual Income: 54.38 k$
- Average Spending Score: 48.85
- Marketing Strategy: Focus on customer loyalty programs, practical products, and personalized discounts.

## Conclusion
K-Means identified five customer segments with different age, income, and spending patterns. These segments can help businesses design targeted marketing campaigns and improve customer engagement.
